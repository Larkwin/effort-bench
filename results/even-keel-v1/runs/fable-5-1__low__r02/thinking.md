I'm outlining the full set of requirements before building the HTML file: unique validated people entries, expense tracking with payer/split logic using integer cents and remainder distribution, balance calculations, a greedy settle-up algorithm, CAD currency formatting, localStorage persistence, and responsive accessible styling with brand colors.

For amount validation, I'm planning to trim and parse the string with a regex allowing up to two decimals, stripping optional dollar signs and commas, then converting to integer cents while rejecting zero or negative values. I'm keeping person removal out of scope and deciding to preserve checkbox and payer selection states across re-renders, defaulting new entries to checked, and resetting all checkboxes after adding an expense. For settling debts, I'm using a greedy approach that matches the largest debtor against the largest creditor repeatedly to minimize the number of transfers.

I'm structuring expense records with id, description, cents, payer, and split array, computing each person's share by dividing total cents evenly and distributing any remainder to the first few people in trip order, then deriving balances as paid minus owed share. I'm also setting up an error message element that gets created and removed dynamically, with specific text for missing description, invalid amount, missing payer, and empty split selection, plus an empty-state prompt telling users to add a person before logging an expense while still keeping the form rendered. Finally I'm adding focus-visible outline styling in the Harbour color and applying that same accent color to checkboxes.
