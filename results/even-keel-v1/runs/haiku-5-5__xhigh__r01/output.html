Even Keel

Split trip costs so everyone ends up even.

People

Names must be unique. Capital letters don't count, so Ana and ana are the same person.

Expenses

Amounts are in Canadian dollars and split equally between the people you select.

Up to 2 decimal places, for example 42.50.

Split between

Everyone is selected by default. Uncheck anyone who did not share this cost. Leftover cents go to the first people added to the trip.

Balances

A positive amount is owed to that person. A negative amount is what they owe.

Settle up

Each payment goes from someone who owes to someone who is owed.
