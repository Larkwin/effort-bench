I'm sketching the visual identity — a harbour navy and signal orange palette on warm off-white paper, pairing a confident serif display face like Fraunces with a clean sans like IBM Plex Sans for body text.

I'm thinking through the tone of money language: treating owed balances as neutral rather than alarming, so debts read in calm slate tones instead of red, reserving the signal color mostly for action buttons and errors. I checked contrast ratios to confirm the signal color meets accessibility standards on white backgrounds for both text and buttons.

I'm now sketching the overall data model and UI logic — people and expenses as simple state objects, a keel/waterline motif for balance bars centered at zero, greedy debt-settlement matching to minimize transfers, and careful input validation for amounts and splits. I'm also working out layout details: a two-column desktop grid with people/expense forms on one side and balances/settle-up/expenses on the other, undo-based deletion instead of confirm dialogs, and accessible test IDs for people, expenses, and balance rows while keeping visible text clean of extra button labels.
