Do you notice a difference between Claude models, and between effort levels?

One prompt for a small web app, sent to four Claude models at four effort levels, five times each. Every app was tested by script, judged side by side by two Claude models, and voted on blind by a person. Now you can vote too.

How the blind test works

  • Each pair is two apps built from the same brief by different settings. The apps are live: add people and expenses if you like.
  • Vote with the buttons or the keys A, B and T (tie). Keep "Stay blind" ticked to hide which model and effort made each app until you're done.
  • Choose "same model, different effort" to test whether you can see what more thinking buys.
  • Your votes stay in your browser. Untick "Stay blind" to see your results next to the judges', or export them.

Reproduce it

Everything is in the repository: the harness, the prompts and rubrics, every generated app with its screenshots, checks and judge reviews, every side-by-side judgment, the blind votes, and the script that recomputes each number in the write-up. See github.com/Larkwin/effort-bench.
